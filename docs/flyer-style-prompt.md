# SeniorPadi event flyer: style prompt for AI

Paste the **Master prompt** into an AI image tool (ChatGPT / DALL·E, Midjourney, Ideogram, Gemini, Canva Magic Media, etc.), then fill in the three `[BRACKETS]` at the bottom. Use the **Example prompts** as a starting point.

## What the app does with the picture (why these rules)

- The event card shows your picture **at its own shape**, so the card fits any flyer exactly: no cropping, no blank bands. The name, date, time, place, price and Register button are shown as text beside or under it, so the picture does not need to repeat them.
- Seniors read on small phones, often in bright light. Keep things big, simple and high contrast.

**Pick one shape and use it for every event, so the cards look alike:**

| Shape | Exact size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Landscape 4:3 (recommended)** | **1200 x 900 px** | gives the shortest, neatest card on phones and laptops, so Register is reached soonest |
| Square | 1080 x 1080 px | also fine |
| Portrait 4:5 | 1080 x 1350 px | works, but the card is taller (the picture stops at a set height) |

JPG, under 1 MB. Don't go taller than 4:5: the card stops a picture at 40 rem tall and shows it whole inside that.
Keep the important part about **60 px away from every edge** (a calm margin), and make words big and bold: phones show the picture at roughly a third of its size.

## Master prompt

```
Create a warm, modern event picture for SeniorPadi, a community app that helps
people aged 50+ in Ibadan, Nigeria find activities and make friends.

FORMAT
- [Choose one: portrait 4:5 at 1080 x 1350 px, square 1080 x 1080 px, or landscape
  4:3 at 1200 x 900 px]. Keep the main subject in the middle 80% and leave a calm
  margin of about 6% on every side, so nothing important is near an edge.

SUBJECT
- Show the activity itself: [ACTIVITY], with [NUMBER] Nigerian / West African
  people aged 55 to 75 who look happy, active and dignified (never frail, never
  stereotyped). Mix of women and men, natural skin tones, everyday colourful
  Nigerian clothing. Real, candid, documentary photography style, not stock-photo
  stiff. Faces and hands must look natural and correct.
- Setting: [PLACE / SETTING, e.g. a bright community hall in Ibadan].

STYLE
- Natural, soft daylight, shallow depth of field, clean uncluttered background.
- Colour mood: friendly and premium. Gentle purple accents (#6221c7), warm sunshine
  yellow and amber, white. Slightly lifted shadows, high clarity.
- Looks good small on a phone: one clear focal point, strong contrast.

TEXT ON THE PICTURE
- [Choose one]
  A) NO TEXT AT ALL (recommended: the app shows the name, date and place itself).
  B) POSTER: only the event name in 2 to 5 words, very large, bold, rounded sans-serif
     (like Inter or Nunito Sans Bold), white on a dark purple (#2a1157) band or dark
     on white, at least 7:1 contrast. Small "SeniorPadi" wordmark in a corner. No other
     text, no tiny print, no phone numbers.

DO NOT
- No watermark, logo of other brands, or stock-site marks.
- No tiny text, busy patterns behind text, or text over faces.
- No alcohol, cigarettes, money, or medical claims.
- No distorted faces or hands, extra fingers, or garbled lettering.

EVENT: [EVENT NAME]
```

## After you generate

1. Check it on your phone at normal size. If you can't read the main idea in one second, simplify it.
2. Make sure there are no spelling mistakes in any text, and faces and hands look natural.
3. In the admin page for the event, upload the picture and write the **picture description** (alt text) in one plain sentence, for people who use screen readers. Example: *"Six smiling seniors doing gentle exercises in a bright hall."*
4. Open the event on a phone and on a laptop to confirm it looks right.
